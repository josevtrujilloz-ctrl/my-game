# Player feedback

## Player 1

- Observed playing; appeared to enjoy the game.
- Found it entertaining, but described the overall design as dated and not visually appealing.
- Wanted to play on a phone.
- The student recalled feedback about the snake's appearance and chose that area for a small improvement.
- Student's chosen direction: a more realistic, scary snake.

Player 1 notes above are paraphrases, not direct quotes.

## Player 2

1. Eat the apples and not crash. The snake got longer, so I figured the goal was a high score.
2. I got a little bored after the first minute because the speed never changed. Also, I didn't know the timer mattered. Was I racing it?
3. If it sped up as I got longer, or if there was something to beat besides my own best score.

## Player 3

1. I thought it was regular snake, just with a creepy look. Eat, grow, don't hit yourself or the wall.
2. I died right away the first time because I couldn't tell which way the head was facing. Then the tongue and eyes helped a lot. The dark green also made the apple stand out, which I liked.
3. Quick restarts. I want to jump back in right after dying. Keeping my best score on screen would make me want to beat it.

## Player 4

1. Get the apple, get bigger. I had no idea what the timer was for.
2. Once the snake got long, it felt crowded and I got stuck going in circles. It stopped being fun and started feeling like survival.
3. Different apples, like a golden one worth more points, or a small surprise now and then. The scary snake look is cool, but the game itself felt the same every round.

## Player 5

1. Eat apples and don't die. It was obvious right away, which I liked.
2. Nothing really stuck me. I got bored around the middle, because the same thing kept happening and nothing changed.
3. Seeing my score go up faster for taking risks, like grabbing apples near the edge. A "new best score!" moment when I beat my record would feel good too.

## Themes and possible follow-up

All five players are represented. Players 2-5 answers were supplied by the student; they were not independently observed by the assistant.

- Timer purpose: explicitly questioned by Players 2 and 4 (two of four new replies).
- Speed: Player 2 explicitly requested increasing speed. Players 4 and 5 requested variety or scoring rewards; these are related but different requests.
- Replay motivation: Players 3 and 5 mentioned best score.
- Appearance: Players 3 and 4 responded positively to the new look. Player 3 reported initial direction confusion, then found the eyes and tongue helpful.
- Restart and a visible best score already exist. Player 3's request may concern discoverability or convenience; ask what felt missing before choosing a solution.

The completed improvement remains the snake redesign. Additional requests are future candidates, not commitments for this project.
